✅ Programming: What's a loop?

        
A loop repeats an action — "try this, check it, try the next one" — until a condition is met. You watched one try every possible key, one at a time, and stop the moment it found the right one.

      

      

        
✅ Quantum: Why won't long passwords stay safe forever?

        
Classical brute force takes exponentially longer as keys get longer — a 128-bit key would take a classical computer far longer than the universe has existed. A quantum computer needs roughly the square root of that effort — which is why banks are switching to longer, quantum-resistant keys now.
