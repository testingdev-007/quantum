Back to the two aims from the start.

      

        
✅ Programming: What's a conditional check?

        
A conditional is a rule that checks something and branches: "if total risk is over the limit, mark it invalid — otherwise, it's fine." You watched that check run live on every combination.

      

      

        
✅ Quantum: Why finding the best combination gets impossible fast

        
Each extra investment option DOUBLES the number of combinations to check. That's exponential growth — the same shape you saw with passwords in the Cipher Room. Quantum computers can explore many combinations using superposition, the same trick as the Vault Heist.
